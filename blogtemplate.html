Invisible Aftershock

Last updated on: 31/08/2026

The Invisible Aftershock is an interactive data visualization platform exploring the overlap between climate change exposure, community characteristics, and mental health vulnerability across the Pacific. The project covers 14 Pacific countries and territories from 2005 to 2021, combining indicators including disaster exposure, temperature and precipitation anomalies, rural population share, and estimated suicide mortality rates. Through interactive maps and Sankey flow diagrams, users can explore patterns across countries and years. The analysis is designed as an exploratory visualization of overlapping indicators rather than an estimate of causal effects.
